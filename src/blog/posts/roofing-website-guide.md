<p>Roofing is one of the most competitive home services in the country. In hail-prone parts of the Midwest, South, and Mountain West, one severe storm can set off thousands of homeowner searches within days. Roofers ready for that traffic book out weeks ahead, and the rest lose those leads to better-prepared competitors.</p>

<p>Here's what a roofing website needs to catch them.</p>

<h2>Make Storm Damage and Insurance Claims a Priority</h2>

<p>For most residential roofers, insurance-claim storm work is a large share of revenue, so speak directly to homeowners whose roof was just damaged. That means:</p>
<ul>
  <li>A dedicated page for "Storm Damage Roof Repair" or "Hail Damage Roof Replacement"</li>
  <li>A clear explanation of the insurance claim process from your side</li>
  <li>Language like "We work directly with your insurance company" if that's true</li>
  <li>A strong call to action: "Get a free storm damage inspection"</li>
</ul>

<p>Homeowners in the middle of a claim are often confused and stressed. A site that explains the process and offers to guide them through it brings in far more calls than a generic "call us for a free estimate" page.</p>

<h2>Show Before-and-After Photos of Every Job</h2>

<p>A homeowner deciding whether to hire you wants to see your work. Before-and-after photos of roofs you've replaced, especially in the styles and materials common in your area, build more trust than any copy.</p>

<p>Photograph every job; phone photos from the ground are fine. Over time, build a gallery that covers different roof styles (architectural shingles, metal, tile), replacements and repairs, and commercial and residential work. The more variety, the more visitors see a roof like theirs.</p>

<h2>Feature Your Manufacturer Certifications and Warranties</h2>

<p>Premium manufacturers such as GAF, Owens Corning, CertainTeed, and Atlas have certification programs that let installers offer longer workmanship warranties. A standard contractor might offer a 5-year workmanship warranty, while a GAF Master Elite contractor can offer 25 years, backed by the manufacturer.</p>

<p>Homeowners who value a manufacturer-backed warranty look for certified contractors, but only if they can find the information easily, so put it front and center.</p>

<h2>Prove Your License, Insurance, and Years in Business</h2>

<p>Roofing fraud and fly-by-night storm chasers are common, and homeowners know it. They look for signs that your company will still exist after their check clears.</p>

<p>Make your legitimacy easy to check:</p>
<ul>
  <li>State contractor license number prominently displayed</li>
  <li>General liability and workers' comp insurance confirmed</li>
  <li>Physical business address (or service area, for mobile businesses)</li>
  <li>Years in business, with an emphasis on being locally established</li>
</ul>

<h2>Explain Replacement Versus Repair on Its Own Page</h2>

<p>Homeowners often don't know whether they need a repair or a full replacement. A page that explains when each makes sense answers a common search, which helps your search rankings (SEO), and it shows you as an expert instead of a salesperson.</p>

<p>A page or blog post such as "How to Know If You Need a Roof Repair or Full Replacement" is widely searched, and it can lead naturally into your process and an offer of a free inspection.</p>

<h2>Separate Commercial Roofing From Residential</h2>

<p>If you do both, treat them as separate parts of the site. Commercial clients, such as property managers, building owners, and general contractors, want commercial project photos, references, bonding details, and experience with flat roofs, TPO, EPDM, and standing-seam metal.</p>

<p>Mixing the two weakens your message to both, so give commercial work its own page or, better, its own section.</p>

<h2>Be Fast When the Storm Hits</h2>

<p>When hail falls, every roofer in the area starts getting calls at once. Homeowners searching "roof damage repair [city]" decide on the spot, and your site has seconds to win the call.</p>

<p>So your site must load fast, and your phone number must be impossible to miss. Your call to action (free inspection, free estimate, or call now) belongs on the first screen visitors see, before any scrolling or paragraph of copy.</p>

<p>The roofers who win the storm surge are ready before the storm arrives, and that starts with a website built to bring in calls when traffic spikes.</p>
